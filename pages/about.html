The core mathematical models powering our estimation engine. We use transparent, real-world formulas rather than black-box algorithms.

🏠 CHB HOUSE

Standard Concrete

FOUNDATION

Material Formula Used
CementNumber of Footings × 2–4 Bags
SandNumber of Footings × 0.12–0.25 cu.m.
3/4 GravelNumber of Footings × 0.18–0.40 cu.m.
16mm Deformed BarNumber of Footings × Standard Reinforcement
Tie WireTotal Reinforcement × 3%

Note: The engine assumes a standard footing size scaled by structural requirements rather than computing L × W × H.

COLUMNS

Material Formula Used

SandNumber of Columns × Constant
3/4 GravelNumber of Columns × Constant
16mm Deformed BarNumber of Columns × Standard Bars

Uses predefined material constants for each column instead of calculating concrete volume.

ROOFING

Material Formula Used
Roofing SheetsRoof Area ÷ Effective Sheet Coverage
C-PurlinsRows per Side × 2 × (Roof Length ÷ 6m)
Ridge RollRidge Length
Roofing ScrewsRoof Area × Screw Density
Silicone SealantRoof Perimeter ÷ Coverage

Purlins are computed by counting rows based on spacing and converting them into standard 6-meter lengths.

CEILING

Material Formula Used

Carrying Channel(Width ÷ 1.20) × (Length ÷ 6)

Blind RivetsCeiling Boards × Standard Fasteners

PAINTING

Material Formula Used
Primer(Wall Area ÷ 30) × 2
Paint(Wall Area ÷ 30) × 2
Concrete PuttyWall Area ÷ Coverage

The factor of 2 represents both wall sides and two coats in the current implementation.
